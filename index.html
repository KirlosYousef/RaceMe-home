Quick answers before the starting gun

Good to know.

What is RaceMe?

RaceMe is a social running app for live head-to-head races. Challenge friends, match with other runners, or use your own past result as the target. Races include progress, results, and in-app rewards.

Does RaceMe track my location?

RaceMe requests location access while you use the app so it can measure distance and draw your route during a race. Route coordinates stay on your device; race progress and results are shared with the match. You can disable saved route history in the app’s Settings.

Can I use RaceMe with Apple Watch?

Yes. RaceMe for Apple Watch can show your match progress and updates while your iPhone tracks your distance. See the App Store listing for current device and OS compatibility.

Is RaceMe free?

RaceMe is free to download and offers optional in-app purchases. Any current subscription price and renewal details are shown in the app before purchase.
