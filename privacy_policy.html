Your route is yours. RaceMe uses iPhone location while a race is active to calculate distance and draw your route. Route coordinates stay on your device. Race distance, progress, and results are sent to the race service so your match can work.

1. Overview

This policy explains how Kirlos Yousef (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the RaceMe iPhone and Apple Watch apps or visit this website. RaceMe lets you compete in running matches, connect with friends, view race results, and track achievements.

We use information to operate those features, keep accounts and matches working, provide support, and understand how the app is used. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use precise route coordinates for advertising.

2. Information we use

Account and profile

Depending on how you sign in and what you add, RaceMe may process your email address, account identifiers, display name, username, profile photo, login method, and the profile details you choose to share. If you sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal address.

Running, race, and social activity

We process race choices, distance, elapsed time, pace or other displayed performance measures, race progress and results, personal records, experience points, achievements, leaderboard position, friend connections, and chat messages. Profile and match details may be visible to other RaceMe users as part of the feature you use.

Location and route

When you grant permission, the iPhone app reads GPS location while you are using RaceMe in an active race. The app uses those samples on your device to calculate distance and draw your personal route. The route history feature can save route coordinates locally in the app on your iPhone; you can turn off saving future routes in Settings. Raw route coordinates are not sent to RaceMe’s race services in this flow. Your race distance and progress are shared so the match can display both runners and calculate a result.

Photos and camera

If you choose to take or select a profile photo, RaceMe accesses the camera or photo library for that action. The image you choose may be uploaded to your profile and displayed to other users in areas such as your profile, friend lists, or chat. Camera and library access can be changed in iOS Settings.

Onboarding and app analytics

RaceMe may process the goals, motivations, experience level, training frequency, preferred running time, and daily distance goal that you provide during onboarding. Product analytics include events such as app sessions, screens viewed, sign-in and onboarding steps, race starts and finishes, feature use, and purchase events, along with app version, platform, login method, and an account or profile identifier when known.

Purchases, notifications, and technical information

Apple and our subscription service provider process purchase and subscription status. RaceMe does not receive your full payment-card number. If you enable push notifications, Apple Push Notification service processes a device token and your notification preferences so updates can be delivered. Our service providers may also receive technical details needed to run the app, such as connection and error information.

3. Location and route privacy

RaceMe requests location access while in use. Location tracking starts for a race, pauses when the app is no longer in use, and stops when the race ends. You can deny location permission, but a GPS race will not be able to calculate your distance correctly.

Your detailed route is processed on your phone and is not sent to the RaceMe race service by the current route-tracking flow. The other race participant may see your race progress and result, but not the raw route coordinates recorded on your phone. Local route saving is optional and can be disabled in the app’s Settings. iOS Settings also lets you change RaceMe’s location permission at any time.

4. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts and profiles.
  • Match runners, run live races, deliver chat and notifications, and show race results.
  • Save progress such as records, experience points, milestones, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Process purchases, manage subscriptions, and provide purchase support.
  • Understand feature use, fix errors, protect the service, and improve RaceMe.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests and meet legal obligations.

5. When information is shared

We share information as needed to operate RaceMe, at your direction, or when the law requires it.

  • Other users: profile information, chat messages, friend activity, and race progress are shared with the people or leaderboards involved in the feature you use.
  • Service providers: RaceMe uses Apple services, Firebase, brainCloud, Mixpanel, and RevenueCat to support sign-in, app services and storage, push notifications, analytics, and in-app purchases. These providers process information for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal and safety: information may be disclosed if needed to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the security and rights of RaceMe and others.
  • Business changes: information may transfer as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

Service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Their handling of data is also governed by their own privacy notices and terms.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and activity information while your account is active and as needed to provide RaceMe, meet legal duties, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Local route history remains on your iPhone until it is removed from the app or the app’s local data is removed.

You can request account deletion in RaceMe under Settings → Delete Account. Deletion removes your RaceMe account information from active systems, subject to limited retention needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction records. Data held in service-provider backups may take additional time to expire under their retention schedules. Deleting your RaceMe account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change location, camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off future local route saving in RaceMe Settings.
  • Update profile details in the app and delete your account from Settings.
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. Rights vary by location and may be subject to legal exceptions.

8. Security, children, and this website

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your account credentials private and tell us promptly if you believe an account has been accessed without permission.

Where local law requires a parent or guardian to approve an account or the processing of a child’s information, that permission must be obtained before the child provides personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has shared information with RaceMe without required permission.

This marketing website is static and does not set analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages, which hosts the website, may process standard connection logs as part of providing and protecting the hosting service.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, our services, or legal requirements change. We will change the date at the top when we publish a revision. Material updates may also be announced in the app where appropriate.
